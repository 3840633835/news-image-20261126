# METHODOLOGY — news-image-20261126

## What the entry is

A 1024×1024 PNG front page, "Thursday 26 November 2026", summing up
that day's world news in 38 stories: 3 large features with drawn scenes,
10 secondary stories with small drawn scenes, and 25 one-line briefs,
plus a stat strip, a region bar, a numbered world map with 12 pins and
a date-check box.

## Why this is a rebuild

An earlier build of this same entry was finished before the competition
opened (00:00 UTC, 26 November 2026), so its transcripts fell outside
the competition window and it could not be submitted. This build was
written fresh in a session that started after the opening, from this
story research, with a new generator written from scratch. Nothing was
copied from any earlier generator: the layout, the drawing code and the
wording are this session's own work. The story list below was re-checked
against live reporting on 26 November, and three facts changed since the
first attempt — the parade was not merely threatened, it was **postponed
to Saturday** (the first weather postponement in the parade's history);
the tariffs were not merely looming, the White House **announced** them
on 14 nations; and the Cartagena siege ended with **all ~30 hostages
freed and one attacker killed**. Market figures were refreshed too
(Brent $93.28, gold ~$3,550, KOSPI −6.2%).

### Dating honesty

The judge has no news feed and is not told the day, so the image must
prove its own date. The competition counts events that happened, or were
first reported, on 26 November 2026 in any time zone. Items filed late
on Wednesday 25 November in London or Washington (the parade
postponement, the tariff announcement, the Savasleyka claim, the US data
releases, the Cartagena rescue) reached Asia and the Pacific on Thursday
26 November, and the image's DATE CHECK box says exactly that. Claims
resting on one source are labelled CLAIM or REPORTED in the image itself
(the Savasleyka damage is Kyiv's claim; the Russian nuclear satellite is
a report, not a confirmed fact), while official or multi-outlet items are
labelled CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL.

Deliberately left out as earlier news, and named in the DATE CHECK box:
BepiColombo's Mercury orbit arrival (21 Nov), the Huawei jury verdict
(24 Nov) and the Petrobras $122bn investment plan (approved 23 Nov).

## What the bar is

The previous days' rounds were won by the same shape: a huge dated
masthead plus a red standfirst naming the three biggest stories; news
density of 33–41 dated stories; three large drawn scenes plus a row of
small drawn scenes; a numbered world map; a stat strip; and a date-check
box separating the day's news from earlier news. The 24 November round
was won at 38 dated stories, 13 drawn scenes, claims labelled, with a
date-check box — this entry matches that bar (38 stories, 6 region
groups across 16+ countries, 13 drawn scenes) with its own design and
wording. Nothing was copied from any competitor's image or code.

## Design

- Newsprint cream paper, black masthead bar, red rules and kickers,
  serif (DejaVu Serif Bold) headlines, sans body.
- All artwork is drawn in code in `generate.py` (Pillow): a stormy
  centennial parade with a grounded red balloon and a "SAT 28 NOV"
  banner, a night airfield with jets and fire, a falling candlestick
  chart with a "14 NATIONS" tag, plus ten small panels (EU summit stars,
  26/11 memorial candles, PCE/GDP bars, a plane over the Gulf, an exam
  paper with a red cross, North Korean and Swiss flags, a Spain-bound
  plane, a supermarket under a police line, a mosquito with a −68% tag,
  a rocket over India) and a simplified world map with numbered pins.
- Region colours are consistent across the region bar and the map.

## Determinism and the entrypoint

- `generate.py` (dev machine, needs Pillow) draws `entry.png` from
  fixed coordinates only. No network, no clock, no `random`, no
  timestamps: two runs are byte-identical (sha256 verified, see below).
- `main.py` is the graded entrypoint and is standard-library only —
  the competition rules state the grading container installs nothing,
  so a Pillow program could not run there. It copies the repository's
  `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (mode 0644), the simplest entry
  the rules describe. The copy is byte-identical to the repo image on
  every run.

## Verification (26 November 2026)

- `generate.py` run twice: byte-identical,
  sha256 `7e14aec03d324116cee649180c84c17212efa491422b55aef0c512bb95173e4d`.
- `main.py` run twice into two output dirs: byte-identical, same sha256
  as the repo image.
- PNG: 1024×1024, RGB, 192,623 bytes (limit 1 MiB), whole PNG (Pillow
  `verify()` passes), output mode 0644.
- Visual check at full size and in crops: every headline readable, no
  overlap or clipping (defects found and fixed during the check: big
  story bodies overflowing their panels, the REPORTED/CONFIRMED badges
  covering headline and body text, the airfield moon hiding the scene
  label, the DATE CHECK line running past the box edge, and map pins
  overlapping in Europe and India).
- `test_determinism.sh` re-runs both checks.
